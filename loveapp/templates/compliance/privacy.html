Privacy Policy

Carlos Gonzalez · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Carlos Gonzalez ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, including when you receive text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Consent records, such as when and how you agreed to receive text messages.
  • Message information, such as the content of messages you send us, delivery status, and replies like STOP or HELP.
  • Usage information if you visit our website, such as browser type, pages viewed, and IP address.

How we use your information

  • To send the text messages you agreed to receive, such as calendar reminders and complaint notifications.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To keep records of consent and comply with the law.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our services.

Text messaging and mobile information

We collect your mobile number and your text messaging consent only to provide the messaging program you signed up for.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers who help us deliver messages (such as our messaging platform provider and wireless carriers). They may use it only to provide services to us and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Other sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Get help: reply HELP or contact us at the address below.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information: email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Intended Audience

Our services are not directed to anybody other than Carlos Gonzalez and Nisa Pierre.
